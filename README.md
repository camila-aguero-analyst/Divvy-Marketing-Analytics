# Divvy Marketing Analytics

## Project Summary

A marketing analytics project using SQL, BigQuery, and Tableau to analyze rider behavior and develop membership conversion strategies for Divvy Bike Share.

## Project Overview

This project analyzes Q1 2025 Divvy bike-share data to identify behavioral differences between casual riders and annual members.

The goal was to develop data-driven marketing recommendations that encourage casual riders to convert into annual memberships.

## Business Problem

Casual riders generate revenue through single-use rides, but annual members provide more predictable long-term value.

Cyclistic's marketing team wanted to understand:

* How casual riders and members use the service differently
* When riders use the service most often
* Which bike types are preferred
* What opportunities exist for membership conversion

## Tools Used

* SQL (Google BigQuery)
* Tableau
* Excel
* Data Visualization

## Key Findings

### Casual riders generate more trips

* Casual riders accounted for 53% of total rides.

### Casual riders take longer trips

* Casual riders averaged 28.0 minutes per ride compared to 20.5 minutes for members.

### Electric bikes dominate usage

* 83% of all rides were taken on electric bikes.

### Weekend demand is strongest among casual riders

* Casual rider activity peaked Friday through Sunday.

## Recommendations

* Target high-frequency casual riders with membership campaigns.
* Promote unlimited e-bike access.
* Launch weekend-focused membership promotions.
* Highlight cost savings and convenience benefits.
